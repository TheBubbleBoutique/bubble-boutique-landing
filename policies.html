Salon Policies

These policies are designed to keep pets safe, ensure fairness for all clients, and allow the salon to operate smoothly and professionally.

1 Appointments & Booking

  • All grooming services are by appointment only.
  • Appointments can be booked via booking link, message, phone, or DM.
  • A valid contact number is required for all bookings.
  • Appointment times are allocated specifically for your dog; late arrivals may result in a shortened groom, cancellation, or late pick-up fee.

2 Cancellations, No-Shows & Late Arrivals

  • A minimum of 24 hours' notice is required for cancellations or rescheduling.
  • Cancellations with less than 24 hours' notice may be charged 50% of the grooming fee.
  • Failure to attend an appointment without notice (no-show) will be charged 100% of the grooming fee.
  • Repeated cancellations or no-shows may require payment in full upfront for future bookings.
  • Late arrivals of more than 15 minutes may need to be rescheduled and charged as a late cancellation.

3 Payment Policy

  • Payment is due on collection of your dog.
  • Accepted payment methods: cash, or bank transfer.
  • Prices may vary depending on coat condition, behaviour, size, and time required.
  • Any additional charges will be discussed where possible but may be applied if extra work is required.

4 Health, Vaccinations & Flea Policy

  • All dogs must be healthy and fit for grooming.
  • Owners must inform the salon of any medical conditions, injuries, allergies, or recent surgery prior to grooming.
  • If fleas are found during your dog's appointment, a £10 flea sanitation fee per dog will be applied. This covers the additional cleaning and disinfecting of equipment, the salon, transport (where applicable), and the time required to protect other dogs in my care.
  • If a dog is found to have a heavy flea infestation, or if grooming cannot be carried out safely due to the level of infestation or the dog's distress, The Bubble Boutique reserves the right to refuse or stop the groom. Owners may be asked to seek appropriate flea treatment from their vet before a future appointment can be booked.
  • Dogs showing signs of contagious illness may be refused grooming to protect other dogs.

5 Behaviour & Handling

  • Dogs must be able to tolerate grooming safely.
  • Aggressive or dangerously stressed dogs may be refused or stopped mid-groom for safety reasons.
  • Muzzles may be used if necessary for safety.
  • Any bites or attempts to bite must be disclosed before grooming.
  • Owners accept responsibility for any injury caused by their dog to staff or equipment.

6 Coat Condition & Matting Policy

  • Matted coats can be painful and unsafe to groom.
  • Severely matted dogs may need to be clipped short for their welfare.
  • Additional charges may apply for de-matting due to increased time, equipment wear, and stress on the dog.
  • The salon will not perform painful or inhumane de-matting.
  • By booking, owners consent to humane coat removal where necessary.

7 Puppy Grooming

  • Puppy grooms are designed to be gentle, positive introductions to grooming.
  • Results may be limited during early sessions to ensure a stress-free experience.
  • Multiple visits may be required to achieve a full groom.

8 Pick-Up & Collection

  • Dogs must be collected promptly once grooming is complete.
  • A late collection fee may apply if dogs are left beyond the agreed collection time.
  • Dogs not collected by closing time may incur additional charges.

9 Injury, Illness & Emergencies

  • While every care is taken, grooming carries inherent risks, especially with elderly, matted, or anxious dogs.
  • Minor nicks, irritation, or clipper rash may occasionally occur.
  • In the event of an emergency, the salon will seek veterinary care if necessary at the owner's expense.
  • Owners accept that the salon is not liable for pre-existing conditions aggravated during grooming.

10 Photographs & Marketing

  • Photos or videos of dogs may be taken for portfolio, social media, or marketing purposes. Please inform the salon if you do not consent to your dog being photographed.

11 Right to Refuse Service

  • The salon reserves the right to refuse or stop grooming services at any time for safety, welfare, or behavioural reasons.

12 Acceptance of Policies

  • By booking an appointment, you confirm that you have read, understood, and agreed to these salon policies.
